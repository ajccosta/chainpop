Chainpop

or join a friend

  1. Your first move drops a 3-dot blob on any empty square.
  2. After that you can only tap your own blobs to add a dot.
  3. At 4 dots a blob bursts, sending one dot to each neighbour and taking them over. Bursts chain.
  4. Lose all your blobs and you're out. Last color standing wins.
